Dear Professor Narayanan,

I am a researcher at Kumoh National Institute of Technology, writing to request
access to the **TILES-2018** dataset for academic research.

**Research purpose.** We are studying whether wearable physiological signals can
predict decrements in task performance among workers in high-demand settings. We
have completed a first study on two public corpora (a wearable exam-stress dataset
and the Hosseini et al. nurse dataset). We can reliably detect high-demand task
episodes from wrist-worn sensors (AUROC 0.93, validated leave-one-subject-out),
but neither corpus allowed us to test the performance question: one has only a
single objective outcome per participant, and the other records self-reported
stress rather than performance.

TILES-2018 is, as far as we can determine, the only dataset combining continuous
multimodal physiological recording with repeated objective job-performance
measures in a real workplace. Specifically, we would use the semi-daily task
performance measures together with the Fitbit and OMSignal streams to test whether
within-subject physiological state predicts within-subject performance deviation.

**Intended use.** Secondary analysis only, for academic publication. We would make
no attempt at re-identification, would model aggregate constructs rather than
profile individuals, and would cite Mundnich, Booth, L'Hommedieu et al., *Scientific
Data* 7, 354 (2020) as requested on your project page.

**On process.** I submitted a data use agreement previously and did not receive a
response. I note the download page states that the agreement and download pipeline
are currently under construction, so I assume my request simply arrived during that
period rather than having been declined. I am glad to complete the DUA again in
whatever form is most convenient, or to have it countersigned by our institution if
that helps.

Thank you for making this dataset available, and for any guidance on the current
access process.

With best regards,

Victor Kanu

## Notes before sending

- **Add your department and title** (e.g. "PhD candidate, Department of ...") under
  your name. A bare name reads as less accountable than a titled institutional one.
- **If you have a supervisor or PI**, cc them. A DUA is usually signed by an
  institution, not an individual, and showing the responsible party up front
  removes a round trip.
- **Keep the AUROC 0.93 line.** It signals you have already done real work and are
  not fishing for a dataset to explore — the single biggest factor in these requests
  being granted.
- **Do not attach anything.** A first-contact email with attachments is more likely
  to be filtered or ignored. Offer the summary PDF if they ask.
- If there is no reply in **two to three weeks**, send one short follow-up on the
  same thread. After that, let it go — Paper 1 does not depend on this.
